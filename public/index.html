Where this is going

This is the first sitting. Everything below is what we build on top of it.

  1. Voice. Full spoken conversation — talk to it, and it talks back.
  2. A real recommendation engine. ISBN & catalog classification, a seminal-works-first taxonomy, similar-author graphs, and a deliberate weight for the fringe.
  3. The wider conversation. The research miner grows up: reviews, critical essays, lectures and podcast discussions found, transcribed, and woven into every conversation — at scale, across the whole shelf.
  4. Author intelligence. Every author profiled from public sources — photo, school, lineage, stance, politics, how they come across in interviews, and where they're contested — so the companion argues from a real understanding of who wrote what. A skeptic agent verifies every finding before it's trusted.
  5. A reader profile that deepens. Durable memory of what drives you, carried across every conversation.
  6. The living library. Auto-sync of purchases, reading status, and notes.
  7. The journey view. A visual map of where your reading has taken you — and the frontier just past it.
